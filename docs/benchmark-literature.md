# Track 1 benchmark literature brief

Owner: Ruthvik. Working report material, 2026-10-14. Scope: task/evaluation design and its limits; this is not the full project related-work review. CLM Appendix D and the released ContextBench source were inspected directly. The three supporting benchmark summaries below are scoped to their primary paper abstracts; they do not imply reproducing or reviewing every experiment in those papers.

## Why ContextBench fits our question

Our experiment asks who should manage an agent's context. We therefore need tasks where input exceeds the active window, management can preserve enough information to succeed, and grading does not require a subjective judge. CLM's ContextBench deliberately streams operations one at a time into one conversation and uses small synthetic operations with bounded required retention. This reduces task-reasoning difficulty while increasing context pressure. Needle Retention measures selective preservation; KV Store adds offloading and exact retrieval of random values. These properties make them useful diagnostics for separating actor capability from context-management capability. [CLM paper, Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37725); [released benchmark](https://github.com/RulinShao/ContextBench).

The released benchmark is now available, superseding our proposal's plan to rebuild it. Using the official generators, instructions, feasibility gates and graders removes arbitrary choices about vocabulary, needle density and answer matching. We pin a commit because released semantics matter: KV uses answers from all per-turn context snapshots, while needles are evaluated in the final context. A single final transcript would disadvantage agents that retrieve an answer transiently and later compact it. This is why our primary metric comes from official snapshots rather than a new end-only grader. [ContextBench protocol and source](https://github.com/RulinShao/ContextBench).

## What adjacent benchmarks add

[RULER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654) extends basic needle retrieval with multiple needle configurations, multi-hop tracing and aggregation under configurable sequence length and task complexity. Its relevance here is methodological: success on a simple needle test does not establish broad long-context capability. Our inference is that KV/needle results can support a claim about controlled context management, but cannot establish general agent reasoning gains. The proposal's combine-values variant could address that limitation later; it remains deferred until the instructor's core grid is complete.

[LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813) evaluates extraction, multi-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates and abstention using 500 questions in scalable chat histories. It also separates memory design into indexing, retrieval and reading. This offers a useful interpretation of KV failures: a wrong answer can arise because information was stored poorly, retrieval missed it, or the actor failed to reproduce the retrieved value. Our current scorer observes the answer rather than identifying which stage failed. A future failure analysis should use tool/manager logs to distinguish these explanations. We do not transfer LongMemEval performance claims to our synthetic setting.

[MemoryAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05257) focuses on incremental multi-turn interactions and identifies accurate retrieval, test-time learning, long-range understanding and selective forgetting as distinct competencies. Its incremental formulation is closer to a growing agent history than a single fixed prompt. For this project, it motivates preserving the streaming protocol and treating retention as only one part of memory quality. Our two tasks do not directly test learning, temporal updating or broad understanding; those are scope limits, not missing core conditions we should add before the agreed pilot.

## Design implications for our study

Use identical seeded episodes for all management conditions so a difference is attributable to the intervention rather than randomly easier records. Include below-budget controls to detect failures even when compression is unnecessary. Require exact common o200k tokenization and report actual measured input pressure; separately enforce the model's serving-tokenizer limit. Reject instances whose individual operations or necessary retained state violate the benchmark's feasibility bounds. These are project design choices grounded in the diagnostic structure of ContextBench, not evidence that any manager will improve performance.

Keep primary scores and diagnostics separate. Official KV matching tolerates a value inside a longer answer; our strict whole-value diagnostic can reveal format or guessing artifacts. Official needle grading normalizes whitespace; an exact-line diagnostic measures stronger fidelity. Gold-oracle success only shows that generated instances and the grading path work. It is not a no-management baseline. Likewise, a missing KV answer is not enough to prove data destruction if the value may still be in the external store.

For the report, compare paired per-seed accuracies within each actor/task/pressure cell, then inspect uncertainty and failures before drawing scaling claims. Measure compute for actor and manager together through the other tracks. Two deliberately simple tasks can isolate a bottleneck, but neither a positive nor a negative result alone establishes performance on realistic long-horizon workflows.

## Draft report paragraph

We evaluate context management with the KV Store and Needle Retention tasks from ContextBench (Shao et al., 2026). Both deliver synthetic input incrementally and bound the information an ideal policy must retain, allowing us to vary context pressure without requiring complex task reasoning. We use the released generators and graders at a fixed revision and share seeded instances across conditions. RULER shows why retrieval diagnostics alone should not be read as general long-context understanding, while LongMemEval and MemoryAgentBench cover broader memory abilities that our controlled tasks do not target. Our goal is therefore to isolate the effect of manager role and capability before extending to more complex agent tasks. [RULER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654), [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813), [MemoryAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05257).
